# Fridge Roast: One Line, Biggest Laugh

A [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition entry — one line, at most 200
characters, roasting a smart fridge: its app, its firmware updates, its
camera, its ice maker, and its opinions about your milk.

## The line

> My smart fridge updated its firmware at 3 a.m., scanned my milk, and emailed me a lecture on expiry dates like a snitch with an ice maker.

(138 characters.)

## How it works

`main.ts` writes that single line to `/output/entry.txt` when run with Node
22.18 or later:

```sh
node main.ts
```
